1. FINAL PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CattleFeedAI is an evidence-based digital cattle feed and silage quality assessment,
livestock nutritional monitoring, hazard alerting, and professional expert consultation
system built for Indian dairy farmers, licensed veterinarians, and veterinary nutritionists.

The platform addresses the critical challenge of livestock health and dairy productivity
caused by sub-standard, mould-contaminated, or nutrient-deficient cattle feeds and silage.
It bridges the gap between field reality and lab diagnostics through a rigorous,
software-first architecture that unifies:
- Farm and dairy livestock record keeping (Gir, Murrah, Sahiwal, Crossbred).
- Commercial cattle feed pellet and silage inventory management.
- Certified laboratory chemistry tracking (moisture, crude protein, fiber, aflatoxin, pH).
- Rule-based multi-parameter quality screening and multi-hazard risk assessment.
- Computer-vision visual surface screening (FastAPI microservice with deterministic fallback).
- Daily ration and feed scheduling management.
- In-app hazard alerting and proactive herd management advisories.
- Closed-loop expert consultation workflow connecting farmers with veterinarians.
- Longitudinal historical herd analytics tracking nutritional and production stability.

This milestone (M13) is the FINAL milestone of CattleFeedAI, delivering full system
hardening, complete cross-milestone regression, strict multi-tenant isolation, verified
scientific boundaries, and production-ready release packaging.

- Default Port: 8000.
- Endpoints:

- Reliability & Fallback Architecture:
  * Spatial Visual Inference: Extracts image metadata, detects surface discoloration, and inspects visual anomalies.
  * High-Availability Fallback: Automatically engages `DETERMINISTIC_VISUAL_SCREENING` if ML model weights
    are unavailable or disabled.
  * No Hallucination Guarantee: Never fabricates predictions or claims ML when fallback is active.
  * Strict Boundary Isolation: Zero chemical predictions (protein, moisture, aflatoxin, etc.) and zero disease diagnoses.

8. SECURITY HARDENING & TENANT ISOLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A dedicated security verification script (`verify_m13_cross_owner_security.mjs`) tested
Farmer A, Farmer B, Expert A, and Expert B across 73 security checkpoints:
- Ownership Isolation:
  * Farmer A can access and mutate own data (200 OK).
  * Farmer B attempting to access or mutate Farmer A's Farm, Animal, Feed Sample, Silage Sample,
    Test Result, Feed Plan, Alert, Sample Image, or Consultation is strictly rejected with 403 Forbidden.
  * Cross-tenant data leakage is completely eliminated.
- Expert Isolation:
  * Expert A can view and respond to assigned consultations (200 OK).
  * Expert B (unassigned) attempting to view, review, or respond to Expert A's consultation is rejected with 403 Forbidden.
  * Expert B attempting to accept an already-assigned consultation is rejected with 400 Bad Request.
- Unauthenticated Requests:
  * Requests without a Bearer token to protected endpoints return 401 Unauthorized.
  * Requests with tampered/invalid JWT tokens return 401 Unauthorized.
- Nonexistent Resources:
  * Requests for nonexistent entity IDs return 404 Not Found.
- Image & File Security:
  * Maximum upload size enforced at 10 MB.
  * Allowed MIME types restricted to image/jpeg, image/png, image/webp.
  * Filenames sanitized; stored on disk using random UUID naming.
  * Corrupted and non-image payloads rejected with 400 Bad Request.


9. SCIENTIFIC SAFETY AUDIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Prohibited Terminology:
  * The term "dietician" is strictly prohibited across all code, tests, and documentation.
  * Qualified professionals are exclusively designated as "Veterinarian", "Veterinary Nutritionist",
    or "Animal Nutrition Expert".
- Physical Sensor / Camera Limitations:
  * The application explicitly declares that ordinary smartphone camera images cannot measure
    crude protein, moisture %, dry matter, fiber, energy value (TDN/NEL), aflatoxin/mycotoxin concentration,
    minerals, urea, or pH.
  * Verified: AI payloads contain ZERO chemical fields.
- Non-Diagnostic Medical Boundary:
  * The animal health screening module identifies potential nutritional and management risks.
  * It strictly does NOT diagnose veterinary diseases, prescribe pharmaceutical treatments, or
    guarantee medical outcomes.
  * Mandatory disclaimers are attached to all assessment, health, AI, and evidence endpoints.

13. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Visual Surface Screening Scope: Computer vision screening evaluates surface characteristics (coloration,
   texture, visible mold clusters) and cannot assess internal batch conditions without physical sampling.
2. Offline Operation: The current software-first release requires network connectivity to reach the Spring Boot
   REST API and MySQL database.
3. Laboratory Testing Dependency: Accurate chemical nutrient profiles require input from standard laboratory testing.
